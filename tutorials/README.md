## What changed from the inference-only tutorial

The previous notebook (`TASK-INFERENCE`) upscaled a synthetic 64×48 scene and reported `sample-sanity` PSNR against its self-made reference. This notebook keeps that scene and its inference contract as Section 5 and adds the adaptation contract around it: a real photograph corpus fetched with per-file pins and paired under a stated degradation, a fidelity protocol in the carried `metrics.py` (PSNR, SSIM, per category), two non-neural baselines, a bounded fine-tuning with the L1 loss, a per-species breakdown, the scene and four held-out crops re-upscaled after adaptation, and an artifact that reloads. The per-image `evaluation_report` helper is unchanged; `pipe.evaluate` is the corpus-level stage (`measured` / `measured-small-sample`).

## Conformance notes

- **Standalone carrier (ST1–ST8):** the primary path performs no clone, repository install, or repository import. Section 2 embeds the repository's three modules verbatim in topological dependency order (each tagged `metadata.dimer.embedded_module`; the only rewrites make `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS_DIR` working-directory-relative and remove internal relative package imports), Section 3 carries the 4-file snapshot manifest inline and asserts it against the module's constants before fetching, and `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE` records the repository, revision and module digests in the exports.
- **Parity (PAR1–PAR4):** `tests/test_notebook_parity.py` and `tools/validate_release_assets.py` fail when the carried modules, the inline manifest, or the inline pins differ from the repository, or when the notebook is not byte-identical to the generator's output.
- **Model acquisition (MOD1–MOD9, MOD12):** `stage_missing_files(WEIGHTS_DIR, allow_download=True)` fetches only the manifest entries a fresh runtime lacks, at the pinned revision (never `main`); `verify_snapshot` re-hashes every entry and asserts the pinned `model.safetensors` digest and byte count before the processor or the model is constructed; `Swin2SRPipeline.from_pretrained(weights_dir=WEIGHTS_DIR)` loads the verified files (`local_files_only=True`, `trust_remote_code=False`) and picks CUDA when it is visible. No cell outside the carried modules calls `huggingface_hub`, `transformers`, `safetensors`, `urllib`, `torch.optim` or the model's processor directly.
- **Data (DAT1–DAT9, ST6):** the default sample is 360 research-grade, CC0-licensed iNaturalist photographs of six bird species (60 per species, one per observer), each pinned in the carried `samples.py` by photo id, byte size and SHA-256 and fetched from `https://inaturalist-open-data.s3.amazonaws.com/photos/<id>/medium.<ext>` (about 39 MB); a cached or fetched file is refused on any mismatch, and every record keeps its observation page and observer login. `read_corpus` makes each photograph a record: `hr` = the centred 192-px crop, `lr` = `degrade(hr)` (bicubic ÷2, JPEG quality 40), `category` = the species; `build_sample_dataset(seed=SPLIT_SEED)` draws 36 / 8 / 16 per species (216 / 48 / 96) and `check_split_disjoint` asserts no high-resolution image (by decoded-pixel digest) is shared. The template adds a second *External access* bullet naming the bucket, the byte count and the CC0 licence.
- **Stages (§4.1):** `fetch_corpus` + `read_corpus` + `build_sample_dataset` / `load_byod_dataset` + `split_dataset` (sample acquisition) → `validate_dataset` per split + `check_split_disjoint` + `observer_overlap` + refusal probes (validation, with three digests) → `validate_inputs` + `upscale` with sanity checks + `evaluation_report` on the synthetic scene (inference contract) → `nearest_baseline` + `bicubic_baseline` + `pipe.evaluate` on the frozen model with the per-species breakdown (baselines) → `pipe.adapt` (bounded fine-tuning) → `pipe.evaluate` on validation and test with the comparison (evaluation) → `upscale` + `evaluation_report` on the scene again and the example panels (inference) → `pipe.save_artifact` → `Swin2SRPipeline.from_artifact` with the parity assertion (export and reload).
- **Score semantics (UNC1–UNC4):** the model emits pixels, no confidence, and never abstains; PSNR and SSIM are reference-based fidelity measures (own numpy implementations; SSIM on luma with an 11-tap Gaussian window and a 5-px border excluded) and neither is a perceptual judgement; no acceptance threshold ships. The scene re-upscaled after adaptation carries a `sample-sanity` verdict that names the missing dispersion.
- **Baselines and evidence (EVAL6/EVAL10/EVAL11):** the nearest-neighbour and bicubic baselines and the frozen model are scored on the same 96 crops as the adapted model, on both measures and per species; every metric is labelled as one seeded split of one sample under one degradation with no dispersion estimate, and the notebook says explicitly that the frozen model scores below bicubic on this input and that the gain is measured under this degradation only.
- **Split integrity (SPL1–SPL8):** the split is seeded and image-disjoint; validation (48 crops) selects the epoch on PSNR and the test split is used for nothing but the final evaluation; observer overlap between splits is printed as an observation, and the notebook states that images from few photographers or sessions must be split by source.
- **Ceilings (VAL6):** `MIN_INPUT_SIDE` (8), `MAX_INPUT_SIDE` (512), `UPSCALE` (2), `HR_CROP` (192), the record bounds (8..5,000), the 16-px multiple and 16..1024 px reference bounds and the id pattern are stated in the Prerequisites and printed before the model runs; four dataset refusals and one input refusal are demonstrated, and training records must share one input size.
- **BYOD (DAT10–DAT19):** `USE_BYOD` defaults to `False` so the sample path never opens an upload dialog; when enabled, one zip is read member by member (names flattened, no `extractall`) or a directory walked, each image centre-cropped to 192 px and degraded exactly as the sample, and the records flow through the same validation, image-disjoint split, baselines, fine-tuning, evaluation, export and reload cells. The expected shape and the privacy guidance are stated in the Prerequisites and in Section 4, and the training split's summary is written to `outputs/swin2sr_super_resolution_train.csv`.
- Licence: the weights are Apache-2.0 and the notebook, README, card and `docs/WEIGHTS.md` say so; the photographs are CC0 1.0 and are not redistributed — the export records the corpus name, release, bucket, byte count, licence and degradation under `corpus`.
- `tools/validate_release_assets.py` performs source validation only. It does not satisfy the clean-runtime execution requirement; a release review must confirm that a recorded clean run in `../docs/release-verification.md` matches the notebook revision under review before the status is promoted to `Release-grade`.

## Outputs

The default notebook path writes (under `outputs/`):

- `swin2sr_super_resolution_train.csv` — the training split's summary table (id, category, sizes, provenance)
- `swin2sr_super_resolution_input_manifest.json` — the inference-contract manifest (schema, per-input observations, one recorded rejection finding)
- `swin2sr_super_resolution_output_frozen.png`, `swin2sr_super_resolution_output_adapted.png` — the synthetic scene upscaled before and after adaptation
- `swin2sr_super_resolution_evaluation_report.json` — baselines, frozen and adapted metrics on validation and test, the per-species comparison, the adaptation history
- `swin2sr_super_resolution_examples/` — four held-out crops as side-by-side panels (input ×2 nearest, bicubic, frozen, adapted, reference)
- `swin2sr_super_resolution_adapter/` — `adapter.safetensors` + `manifest.json`
- `swin2sr_super_resolution_result.json` — `NOTEBOOK_SOURCE`, model identity, snapshot, corpus and degradation, inference-contract reports, comparison, artifact digest, reload parity, runtime

The earlier record for the superseded `TASK-INFERENCE` carrier is kept in `../docs/release-verification.md`.
